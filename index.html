What we offer

Your property's natural assets — understood as a portfolio.

RAEnvironmental helps property owners, buyers, estate agents, agricultural lenders, investors, land managers and professionals responsible for financing or evaluating rural property and farm improvements to identify, understand and strategically use the natural assets of a property for its economic benefit. The Strategic Natural Asset Portfolio brings individual resources together, evaluates how they interact and identifies opportunities to protect, enhance and use them more effectively.

Almost five decades of experience, supported by specialist knowledge

Our experience spans natural systems management, biodiversity conservation, project planning and implementation, ecological rehabilitation, fire and vegetation management, wetlands and catchments, wildlife management, monitoring, training, facilitation and sustainable land management across South Africa and elsewhere in Africa.

A Network of Specialist Expertise

No single person or organisation can provide specialist expertise across every natural asset.

RAEnvironmental therefore works as part of a network of experienced professionals led by Ken Coetzee of Conservation Management Services. The network enables the appropriate specialist knowledge to be brought into a project according to the natural assets identified and the needs of the property.

RAEnvironmental's role as Natural Asset Strategist is to identify and understand the property's natural assets, recognise where specialist input is required, and integrate that knowledge into a coherent Strategic Natural Asset Portfolio.

Professional services

Natural Asset Strategy — and the professional services that make it work.

The Strategic Natural Asset Portfolio is our flagship service. The individual disciplines and practical skills developed through decades of environmental work remain available as stand-alone professional services.

Management & Project Plans

Development and review of environmental, biodiversity, rehabilitation, invasive alien plant, fire and natural-resource management plans, together with practical implementation programmes, priorities, budgets and schedules.

Implementation & Follow-up

Turning plans into action through project coordination, contractor and team guidance, field follow-up, problem solving and practical adjustment as conditions change.

Monitoring & Progress Auditing

Independent monitoring and auditing of progress against approved plans, objectives, targets, budgets and management commitments, with clear reporting and recommendations for corrective action.

Biodiversity & Natural Systems

Vegetation, habitats, wildlife, ecological corridors and conservation significance assessed and managed as functional natural assets.

Water, Wetlands & Catchments

Water resources, wetlands, catchment condition, runoff, erosion, water harvesting and water security considered as natural infrastructure.

Land, Vegetation & Fire Management

Invasive alien plants, veld condition, fire ecology, erosion and rehabilitation addressed through practical management strategies and implementation.

Project Management & Facilitation

Environmental project leadership, stakeholder and landowner facilitation, specialist coordination, contractor management and the integration of environmental objectives with operational realities.

Climate, Risk & Resilience

Rainfall, drought, fire, renewable-resource potential and climate-related risks incorporated into practical land and property strategies.

Training & learning

Lecturing, Training, Mentoring & Facilitation

RAEnvironmental's professional work is supported by extensive experience in lecturing, field training, facilitation, mentoring and competency-based learning. Experience in practice

Projects that shaped our Natural Asset Strategy approach.

Our present approach grows out of decades of practical work where water, biodiversity, land use, people, infrastructure and economics had to be considered together rather than as separate environmental subjects.

Nuwejaars Wetland Special Management Area

A landscape-scale model integrating private landowners, wetlands, biodiversity, sustainable farming, wildlife, fire, alien vegetation control and tourism across the Agulhas Plain.

Buchu Bush Camp & De Hoop

Practical experience linking conservation, tourism, infrastructure and neighbouring communities — demonstrating how natural assets can support sustainable economic opportunity.

Wetlands, Wildlife & Biodiversity

Long-term work in catchments, biodiversity management, wildlife, monitoring, rehabilitation and landscape management provides the practical foundation for evaluating natural assets.

Current African work

Wells for Zoë — Malawi

Working in the Mzuzu region within miombo landscapes on erosion, land rehabilitation, tree establishment and sustainable land-use opportunities. The work explores how degraded natural systems can be rebuilt into productive, resilient natural assets that support communities and livelihoods.
