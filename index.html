What we offer

Your property's natural assets — understood as a portfolio.

RAEnvironmental helps property owners, buyers, estate agents and land managers identify, understand and strategically use the natural assets of a property for its economic benefit.

Every property has a portfolio of natural assets. Many are present but have never been identified, assessed or considered as part of the property's overall asset portfolio. Our Strategic Natural Asset Portfolio brings these assets together, evaluates their potential and identifies opportunities to protect, enhance and use them more effectively.

Experience supported by specialist knowledge

RAEnvironmental brings almost five decades of practical experience in conservation, environmental management, land management and ecological project leadership across South Africa and Africa.

No single person can be an expert in every natural asset. We therefore work through a network of experienced professionals and specialists, bringing in the appropriate expertise when required — from biodiversity, wildlife and soils to water, wetlands, climate, renewable resources, fire, rehabilitation and conservation management.

The result is not simply another environmental report. It is an integrated portfolio of the property's natural assets, supported by the appropriate professional knowledge and translated into a practical strategy.
